Prepress Guidelines & FAQ

What bleed size should I include in my artwork?

We recommend a standard 3mm (0.125 inch) bleed on all outer edges for cut-to-bleed documents. Keep all vital typography and logos at least 4mm inside the trim line.

Which color profile should I export?

For best color fidelity, convert artwork to CMYK (GRACoL 2006 or FOGRA39). If RGB images are submitted, our automated Raster Image Processor converts them using spectrophotometer ICC profiles.
